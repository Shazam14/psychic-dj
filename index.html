01 — What we do

Maybe not yours exactly. But close enough.

04 — What we build on our own

Not everything we work on
is someone else's problem.

One of the things we are building is our own. It is about getting around the country — which roads are passable, by what, and when — so that the person waiting by the highway knows what is coming, and whether it can reach them at all.

It is built for commuters. Not for operators, not for anyone's dashboard. For the people who lose a morning to a road nobody told them about.

We tried it once in 2024 and it did not ship. We ran into a regulatory wall we could not get through, and no amount of engineering was going to move it. So we stopped, wrote down honestly why it failed, and left it alone.

We are building it again now, on a model that does not need permission we cannot get.

We mention it because it is the most useful thing we can show you. Anyone can describe the work that went well.
